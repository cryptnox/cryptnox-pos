const char* pos_asset_t::caption

Caption over the contract address, on the confirm screen and the Tx tab. A network's own coin has no contract to show, so its row names the asset instead. Spelled out per asset rather than derived from the ticker: the Tron rows say "Token contract" where the EVM ones name the token, and that is a wording decision, not a rule.
