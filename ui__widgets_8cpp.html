Screen title, centred but never underneath the top-left icon button.

The title gets the gap between two symmetric gutters; if it does not fit at 20px it drops to the 14px face rather than overlapping the icon or eliding. The width cap and LONG_DOT are the backstop for a title too long even at 14px.
