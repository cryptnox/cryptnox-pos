Detailed Description

Names for one network, in both deployments.

The long forms name the deployment, because "Ethereum" and "Ethereum Sepolia" differ by the only thing that decides whether a sale settles in money. The mainnet forms carry no suffix on purpose — a production terminal should not be shouting a word nobody needs, and the testnet ones then stand out.
