216/* Touch calibration. Two targets: two points are the whole of the linear map
217 * touch_to_screen() applies.
218 * ponytail: two-point linear. If a panel turns out skewed rather than offset
219 * and scaled, the upgrade is an affine map and four corners.
220 *
221 * Steps 0 and 1 capture a corner each; step 2 applies the result live and asks
222 * the operator to confirm by tapping with it, so a calibration that makes Save
223 * unreachable is never stored, and the deadline restores the old numbers. */
